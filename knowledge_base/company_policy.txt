SHOPEASE CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

COMPANY INFORMATION

ShopEase is an online shopping platform that provides products and delivery services to customers.

PRODUCT RETURNS

Customers can return an eligible product within 30 days of delivery.

The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

Customers can request a return after delivery if the return request is made within 30 days of delivery.

DAMAGED PRODUCTS

If a product is damaged when the customer receives it, the customer can return the damaged product within 30 days of delivery.

The damaged product should be returned in its original packaging.

Customers can contact customer support if they need help with the damaged product return process.

PRODUCT EXCHANGE

Customers can request an exchange for an eligible product within 30 days of delivery.

The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

REFUNDS

Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are issued after the returned product is approved.

Customers can contact customer support if an approved refund has not been received within 7 business days.

SHIPPING

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days.

Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

Delivery time may depend on the selected shipping method.

ORDER TRACKING

Customers can track their orders using the tracking link provided in their order confirmation email.

The tracking link is provided after the order has been confirmed.

If the tracking link is not available, customers should contact customer support.

ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers can request order cancellation before the order is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

Customers can contact customer support for help with cancellation requests.

PAYMENT METHODS

ShopEase accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and Cash on Delivery for eligible orders.

Cash on Delivery availability may depend on the product and delivery location.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.

Customers can contact human customer support during business hours.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT CONTACT

Customers who need human assistance can contact the ShopEase customer support team during business hours.

ESCALATION POLICY

The AI customer support agent must answer questions using only the information available in the ShopEase knowledge base.

The AI agent must not guess or invent information.

If the knowledge base does not contain enough information to answer a question, the AI agent must inform the customer that the information is unavailable.

The question should then be escalated to human customer support.

The AI agent should provide a direct answer whenever the required information is available in the knowledge base.
